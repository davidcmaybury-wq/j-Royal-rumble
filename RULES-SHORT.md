**J! ROYAL RUMBLE**
Thirty players. Three start. One survives.

**Getting in** — everyone draws an entry number. Three players open; the rest come in one at a time. Your buzzer counts down your last three clues.

**Getting it right** — every other player in the ring pays you the clue's value. Four opponents on a $400 clue is **+$1,600 to you, −$400 to each of them.** Everyone bleeds, all match.

**Getting it wrong** — you lose the value and you're locked out of that clue. It goes back out to everyone else. If one of them takes it, **you pay them too.**

**Nobody gets it** — everyone in the ring loses half.

**Below zero and you're out.** Zero is still alive.

**There's a ceiling on your score, and it falls all match.** Late entrants can't just walk in and outlast a worn-down field.

**Waiting your turn? Buzz anyway.** Your buzzer works from the moment you join. It doesn't score, but it reports your time and where you'd have placed. The people in the ring have been warming up for twenty minutes — you shouldn't arrive cold.

**Last one standing wins.** Clues survived, toss outs, points drained, buzzer speed — all tracked, all posted, none of them the winner.

⚠️ Don't jump the lights. Buzzing early costs you 250ms, and 250ms is a lifetime here. Good players don't react to the lights anyway — they learn the rhythm of the read. A perfect buzz reads 0.0.

**To play:** open the buzzer link, enter the four-letter room code, add your name, and leave the page open. That page is your buzzer.
