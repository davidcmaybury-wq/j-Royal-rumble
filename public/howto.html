J! HOW TO PLAY

Never done this before? Nothing here is assumed. Five steps and you are in.

1

Get everybody on a call

Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Discord — whichever your group already uses. The game does not run the call; it runs alongside it. The host reads the clues aloud, so everyone needs to hear each other.

Any call will do. The host talks; everybody listens.
2

The host starts a game and reads out the code

One person presses Host a match on the front page. They get a four-letter room code — they read it out on the call, or paste the join link into the chat.

Said aloud, or pasted in the chat. Either works.
3

Everybody else opens their buzzer

Go to the front page, press Play, type the code, pick a name. Your phone or laptop is now your buzzer. Leave that page open — it is your buzzer for the whole match.

It waits, then goes green. Tap the circle or press the space bar.
4

Somebody needs to show the board

Two ways, pick either:

If the host shares their screen on the call, everyone watches the board there and just uses their phone to buzz.

If nobody is sharing a screen, each player presses Show the board here on their buzzer and gets the board alongside it. That needs a laptop or desktop — the board does not fit on a phone.

Left: watch the shared screen, buzz on your phone. Right: the board and the buzzer in one window.
5

Play

The host reads a clue, then arms the buzzers. Five lights count down. Fastest thumb takes it — answer out loud on the call and the host rules yes or no.

Get it right and every other player pays you the clue's value. Get it wrong and you lose it, and they can steal it. Drop below zero and you are out. Last one standing wins.

⚠ The space bar only works when the buzzer window is in front

This catches people out. If you have clicked over to the video call, or to another tab, your space bar goes to that window instead and your buzz never happens. Click once on the buzzer page to bring it back, and you are armed again.

Safest habit: tap the big circle rather than using the space bar. It cannot be stolen by another window, and it is exactly as fast.
